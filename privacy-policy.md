# Privacy Policy for MeetCost

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2025

**Contact:** veltosais@gmail.com

## Overview

MeetCost ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices.

## Data We Collect

**We collect ZERO personal data.**

MeetCost does not:
- Collect personal information
- Track user behavior
- Use analytics services
- Transmit data to external servers
- Require account creation or login
- Access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location
- Share data with third parties

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device** using:
- iOS UserDefaults
- Local device storage only

Your data:
- ✅ Stays on your device
- ✅ Never leaves your iPhone/iPad
- ✅ Is not backed up to our servers (we have none)
- ✅ Is not shared with anyone
- ✅ Is deleted when you delete the app

## What Data Stays Local

The following data is stored locally on your device:
- Employee names and hourly rates
- Meeting records (name, duration, cost)
- Team groups and organization
- Meeting notes
- App settings and preferences

## Third-Party Services

MeetCost does **not** integrate with any third-party services, including:
- No analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
- No advertising networks
- No crash reporting
- No cloud storage providers

## Children's Privacy

MeetCost does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Our app does not target or market to children.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Your device's built-in security protects your data
- We recommend keeping your device password-protected
- Enable biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) for additional security

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:
- **Access:** View all data within the app
- **Delete:** Delete individual meetings or employees
- **Export:** Share meeting reports
- **Complete Deletion:** Delete the app to remove all data

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected (none in our case)
- Request deletion of personal information (all data is local)
- Opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't collect or sell data)

Since we collect no personal data, these rights are automatically satisfied.

## European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users in the European Union have the right to:
- Access their personal data (all data is local, viewable in-app)
- Rectification of inaccurate data (edit in-app)
- Erasure ("right to be forgotten") (delete app)
- Data portability (share/export features)

Since we collect no personal data and store everything locally, these rights are automatically satisfied.
