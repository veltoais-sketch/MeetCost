## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add team members?
1. Open the app
2. Tap the "Team" tab
3. Tap "+" to add a new team member
4. Enter their name, role, and hourly rate

### How do I start tracking a meeting?
1. Tap "Start Meeting" on the Home tab
2. Enter meeting name
3. Select participants
4. Tap "Start" to begin tracking

### Why does the app need calendar access?
Calendar access is optional and helps you quickly import upcoming meetings. The app works fully without calendar access. Your calendar data is never stored or transmitted.

### Is my data private?
Yes! All data stays on your device. We don't have servers, accounts, or any way to access your data.

### How do I delete my data?
Simply delete the app from your device. All data is stored locally and will be removed.
